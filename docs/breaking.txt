# Breaking changes per release: `version | what changed, what to do`.
# scripts/changelog.py lists them first in the release's CHANGELOG.md
# section (docs/releasing.md). Changes to what docs/api.md calls "Not API"
# are not listed.
0.4.0 | `s2_sim::Rng` is the Park-Miller generator of SIM-RNG (`draw`, `range`, `fraction`, `reset`, `state`, `seed`, `draws`; it was a xorshift with `new` and `next_u64`); every game draws other numbers than with 0.3.0, so its states and state hashes differ (docs/api.md, change notes)
0.4.0 | `s2_ai::AiSystem::new(config, &world)` takes no seed: the computer players draw from the simulation's generator (AI-2.7)
0.4.0 | `s2_ai::AiSystem::activate` and `activate_all` take `&mut World`: the activation takes object numbers from the game's counter (SIM-START-4.2)
0.4.0 | `s2_ai::AiSystem::tick(world, rng, numbers)` is the closure of `World::step_then` (the generator and the object counter lent); `AiSystem::step(&mut world)` runs it
0.4.0 | `s2_viewer::Extensions` has more fields (the hooks of docs/extending.md): end a struct literal with `..Default::default()`
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Requester` has a new variant `Unlinked` (ECO-REQ-2.3a: a request whose link was cancelled, MIL-SOL-4.4, waiting in the queue for its turn); `World::requests` can hold it, and a `match` on `Requester` needs an arm for it
0.5.0 | `s2_ai::expansion::Expansion::placed` is `remembered` (AI-MIL-3.2, 4.1: the target remembered by the military run that made an order)
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Road` has a new field `number` (SIM-START-4.2: its object number, the order of a flag's roads, TRN-FREE-6.1); a struct literal of it needs the field
0.5.0 | `s2_ai::plan::Need` has a new field `tied` (AI-CON-8.3 b: its place in its consumer's need list); a struct literal of it needs the field; the game snapshot version is 11
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Order` has a new field `made` (ECO-REQ-5.3f: its place in the order the orders were made) and `s2_sim::Ware` a new field `order` (the order it serves, filed with it until it is delivered); a struct literal of either needs the field
0.5.0 | `s2_ai::sites::route_lengths` is gone: `route_length(world, start, to)` gives the length of one route, a free-walk path (AI-CON-5.4, Q-281); the game snapshot version is 14
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Work::Back` has a field `score`, `Work::StandBack` and `Work::Return` a field `score` too, and `Work` new variants `WaitBack` (ECO-OUT-3.8a: an outdoor worker carrying a ware back asks for its place at the flag) and `Off` (ECO-WORK-3.1b: a worker while production is switched off); a `match` on `Work` needs `Back { .. }` and arms for `WaitBack` and `Off`; the world snapshot version is 43
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Approach::Walking` is `{ clock, back, out_ticks }` (was `{ clock, total }`) and `s2_sim::Builder` has a new field `walk`, a `movement::LatticeWalk` (TRN-MOVE-2.5: each lattice step's rate set when it starts); `Builder::starts` holds `construction::NOT_YET` until a unit's walk to that position has ended; the world snapshot version is 41
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Ware` has a new field `next_after` (TRN-CARRY-5.4: the flag it takes after a flag G, remembered for the last-place test and the road swap); a struct literal of it needs the field; the world snapshot version is 44
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::LeavingStep` has a new variant `StepOut` (MIL-SOL-4.5: a released soldier's step from the building's field to its flag); a `match` on it needs the arm; the world snapshot version is 45
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::LeavingStep` has a new variant `Through { to, done, progress_bits, rate_bits }` (TRN-FREE-6.4b T34: a removed site's builder finishes his work step and walks on to the flag's point); a `match` on it needs the arm; the world snapshot version is 47
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Settler` has a new field `list`, a `s2_sim::SettlerList` (ECO-REQ-0.2: the list of the six the settler was made in, which the settlers' part of a tick updates in order); a struct literal of it needs the field; the world snapshot version is 48
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::LeavingStep::Through` is `{ walk, home }` (was `{ to, done, progress_bits, rate_bits }`): TRN-FREE-6.4b T34 (Q-286), a removed site's builder walks his whole work walk on, a `movement::LatticeWalk`, then the line to the flag's point (`home`); the world snapshot version is 49
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Building` has a new field `productivity_bits`, the float32 statistics value of ECO-OUT-3.11 (AI-MIL T24); a struct literal of it needs the field; the world snapshot version is 50
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Work` has a new variant `OutNext { spot }` (ECO-FOOD-4.1a T51: the fisher's trip started, his walk out in the next tick); a `match` on it needs the arm; the world snapshot version is 51
0.5.0 | the world snapshot version is 52 (ECO-DIST-3.1a T30: each player's order groups in the order of their first orders)
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::Ware` has a new field `free_place`, its place in its player's free-ware list of its good (ECO-REQ-5.4a); a struct literal of it needs the field; the world snapshot version is 53
0.5.0 | `s2_sim::ReleasedStep::Unnoticed` is `{ walking, to_road }` (TRN-CARRY-7.1a T45: a carrier on its way to a removed road whose step ends in the notice tick stands one tick more); a pattern naming its fields needs `to_road`; the world snapshot version is 54
0.5.0 | `s2_viewer::SettingsOptions` has a new field `profile_dir` (a project's copy of the profile file, docs/extending.md "Settings files"); a struct literal of it needs the field or `..Default::default()`
0.6.0 | `s2_sim::RestoredBuilding` has a new field `soldiers`, the professions of the soldiers a saved military building names (FMT-SAV-3.4a, Q-299), and `s2_sim::RestoreReport` a new field `soldiers`; a struct literal of either needs the field
0.6.0 | `s2_sim::snap::Snap` has a new required method `shape` (the format written out, `s2_sim::snap::Shape`); `snap_struct!` and `snap_enum!` write it, an `impl Snap` by hand needs it. A save's file format moved from the viewer to `s2_ai::save`
0.6.0 | from this version on a save of ours loads in every later version: a change of the snapshot's bytes raises `s2_sim::snap::VERSION`, says what an older game has on the type's macro line (`name @ N = default`) and adds a fixture (`scripts/save-format.sh`, docs/snapshot.md "Changing the format"); a project's own state in a save does the same
0.6.0 | `s2_assets::sav::SavedComputer` holds the computer player's records (FMT-SAV-3.10a to 3.10g) and is no longer `Eq`; a struct literal of it needs the new fields or `..Default::default()`; `s2_ai::AiSystem::take_over(world, save)` takes them over after `activate_all`
0.6.0 | `s2_sim::World::restore` sets the game clock from the save's tick (ECO-FOOD-5.11b; it stayed at 0), so a restored world's `game_seconds` and state hash differ from 0.5.0's
0.7.0 | `s2_sim::Restore` has new fields `wares` (the wares lying at flags, FMT-SAV-3.6, 3.9), `random` (the generator's state and seed, FMT-SAV-3.2) and `statistics` (each player's 8 + 14 series, FMT-SAV-3.11), and `s2_sim::RestoreReport` new fields `wares` and `wares_skipped`; a struct literal of either needs them or `..Default::default()`. A restored world's generator, statistics, wares and so its state hash differ from 0.6.0's
0.7.0 | `s2_assets::sav::SavedFlag` has a new field `packages` (the flag's `Package Container`) and `s2_assets::sav::SaveGame` new fields `building_need_goods` and `statistics`; a struct literal of `SavedFlag` needs the field

0.8.0 | `s2_sim::Rules` has a new field `recipes` (a project's recipe per building type, docs/extending.md "New building types") and `s2_rules::model::Building` a new field `inputs`; a struct literal of either needs it or `..Rules::empty()` / `..Default::default()`.
