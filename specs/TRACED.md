## Recording plan (plan step 7.8h, 2026-10-27)

Every recording so far (E-trace-001 .. 011) is one game: MP_2P_Storm_Coast, player 0 a
human who gives no command, player 1 a computer player of strength 0 (profile 1, Weak),
both Romans, start resources Medium, endless, fog of war on. What that game reaches, from
E-trace-002 (150 000 ticks; settlers, buildings, flags, roads every 10th tick) and the Weak
profile of `ai.lua`:

- **Reached by 150 000:** the whole build-up economy: wood, planks, stone (quarries and
  from 60 000 stone mines), water, grain, flour, bread, beer, fish (one fisher, from
  25 000), meat from hunters and (from about 105 000) the piggery and slaughterhouse, coal and
  iron mines with the smeltery (from 23 000), tools (locksmithery), weapons (two smithies)
  and the recruiting of privates; donkey breeding (from 31 000) and donkeys on 105 roads;
  military buildings (guard houses, towers, one barracks) and their upgrades; harbours,
  the shipyard (from 34 000), ships and an overseas settlement; a storehouse; the computer
  player's tear-downs (woodcutters, hunters, stone pits, 9 coal and 3 iron mines,
  presumably where ore, trees or game ran out; not followed), burning ruins.
- **Never in 150 000:** any attack, fight, conquest or defeat (player 0 is never attacked
  and never does anything; the Weak profile plays the safe military mode); catapults
  (disabled in the Weak profile); coins and promotions (no gold mine, no mint: the Weak
  profile allows 0 mints and no coin arrangement, every soldier stays a private); the
  fortress and the lookout tower; geologists and scouts (no specialist ever); water roads
  and boat carriers; the street optimizer (disabled in the Weak profile); the Africans and
  the Chinese; any other map, player count, start resources, victory condition or fog
  setting; loading a saved game; campaign missions and tutorials.
