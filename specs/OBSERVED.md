# Observations of the original game

Behaviour seen while playing the original (The Settlers II: 10th Anniversary),
not traced in the executable. The implementation builds from a line here only
where the specs are silent, and raises a Q-1xx in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` for the
exact numbers. A spec section, once written, overrides the line. The photos are kept privately with Jens's reference pictures.
